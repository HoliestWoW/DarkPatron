# Dark Patron

**A structural challenge and procedural bounty system for World of Warcraft (Classic Era & Burning Crusade Classic)**

Dark Patron completely overhauls the standard progression loop of World of Warcraft. Upon entering Azeroth, your character is bound by "The Veil"—a strict set of structural limitations that lock away basic game features, higher-tier gear, and even your talent points. To survive, you must accept and execute procedural contracts issued by the Dark Patron, earning the currency required to buy back your freedom. 

Designed with grueling restrictions in mind, this addon seamlessly supports standard, Hardcore, and Solo Self-Found (SSF) playstyles, dynamically adjusting to your ruleset.

## 📜 The Core Loop
1. **Accept Pacts:** Open the Patron's Ledger to view the Bounty Board. Drag and drop randomized contracts into your Active Pacts slots.
2. **Execute the Contract:** Fulfill the conditions of the pact in the open world, in dungeons, or in PvP.
3. **Earn Currency:** Successfully completing pacts rewards **Dark Favor**, **Dark Sigils**, or **Apex Sigils**, depending on the rarity and difficulty of the task. 
4. **Purchase Sanctions:** Rest at an Inn or Capital City to access the Patron's Bazaar. Spend your hard-earned currency to permanently unlock gameplay features, gear tiers, and talent points.

## ⚔️ Procedural Pacts
The Bounty Board dynamically generates contracts tailored to your character's level, class, and professions. Pacts scale in difficulty and reward, and include:
* **Combat Trials:** Execute targets, survive massive damage, successfully parry/dodge, or fight unarmed and unarmored.
* **Elemental Mastery:** Deal specific quotas of Frost, Shadow, Nature, Arcane, Holy, or Fire damage. 
* **Harsh Constraints:** Endure "Flawless" kills (taking zero damage) or uphold the "Grimoire's Vow" (forbidding the cast of any Shadow magic during an execution). 
* **Hoarding & Economy:** Utilize your gathering and crafting professions to stockpile required trade goods, cook specific meals, or amass copper from the world.
* **Targeted Hunts:** Spend Favor to sign Blood Contracts, tasking you with hunting down specific Elite enemies in your local zone.
* **The Apex Hunt:** Group up to conquer Dungeon and Raid bosses to earn highly coveted Apex Sigils. 

## ⚖️ The Patron's Bazaar & The Veil
You begin the game restricted to Common (White) gear, with no access to the Bank, Auction House, Mailbox, or even your own Talent Tree. Attempting to bypass the Veil will result in the immediate removal of illegal gear, the stripping of unsanctioned World Buffs, or forceful dismounting.

You must buy back your power through Sanctions in the Bazaar:
* **Armaments:** Unlock the right to equip Uncommon (Green), Rare (Blue), and Epic (Purple) gear.
* **Grant of Knowledge:** Purchase the right to spend your earned talent points, culminating in the "Capstone Awakening."
* **Worldly Access:** Buy the Hoarder's Key (Bank), Merchant's Writ (Auction House), and Courier's Seal (Mail). 
* **Cavalry:** Earn the right to summon Journeyman, Master, Expert, and Artisan mounts. 
* **Alchemist's Sight:** Unlock the ability to use powerful combat consumables like Limited Invulnerability Potions and Flasks of Petrification. 

## 📖 The Chronicle
Every action is recorded. The Ledger tracks your total pacts accepted, broken promises, and your preferred "Archetype" based on how you fulfill your contracts. For those braving a one-life journey, falling in combat generates a permanent, personalized Death Epitaph detailing exactly where and how your soul was claimed by the Void. 

## ⚙️ Features & Integration
* **Custom UI:** Features a draggable on-screen tracker with a dynamic "Streak Wick" timer that rewards combo bonuses for completing multiple pacts rapidly.
* **Chat Integration:** Shift-click any active or available pact to link a custom, color-coded tooltip directly into the game chat for others to see. 
* **SSF Detection:** Automatically detects Solo Self-Found buffs/addons and restricts access to forbidden Bazaar purchases (like the Auction House or Mailbox) to preserve run integrity. 

---
### Slash Commands
* `/patron` - Toggles the Patron's Ledger.
* `/patron bg` - Toggles the background on the on-screen active pact tracker.
